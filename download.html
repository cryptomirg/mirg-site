Installing on a Mac

Not sure which Mac you have?

Click the Apple menu at the top left of your screen and choose About This Mac. If it says "Chip: Apple M…", pick Apple Silicon. If it says "Processor: Intel", pick Intel.

Steps

Open the file you downloaded, drag Mirg into the Applications folder, then open Mirg from Applications. The first time, macOS asks whether you are sure you want to open an app downloaded from the internet; choose Open.

Using other engines

Web and phone games need nothing else. For Godot, Unity, Roblox and the rest, Mirg shows what is installed and how to get what is missing, under "See what's installed" on its home screen.
