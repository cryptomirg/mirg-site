Questions

What counts as usage?

Each time the agent thinks, writes code or looks at your game, it uses an AI model, and that has a cost. Your plan includes an amount of that each month. Bigger games and more capable models use it faster. Settings shows how much of your plan you have used.

What happens when I run out?

The app tells you, and nothing is charged. You can wait for your plan to renew, or upgrade. On Max you can choose to turn on extra usage, which charges your card $20 at a time, only up to a monthly limit you pick. It is off unless you turn it on.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from Settings → Manage billing. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for. Your projects are files on your own computer and stay yours.

Do I need an API key or any other account?

No. You sign in with your email and that is all. Engines such as Unity or Roblox Studio need their own free accounts if you choose to use them.

Which computers does it run on?

Mac today, both Apple Silicon and Intel, on macOS 12 or later. Windows and Linux are on the way.
