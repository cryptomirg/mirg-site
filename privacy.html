Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what Mirg Properties LLC ("Mirg", "we") collects when you use the Mirg app, account service and website, why, and who receives it. The short version: your projects stay on your computer; what you ask the agent, and the parts of your project it needs, are sent to an AI model provider to get a response; we keep your email, your plan and a record of how much you used.

What stays on your computer

  • Your projects: all game files, code and assets.
  • Your chat history with the agent, and the snapshots used by Restore.
  • Your sign-in session, stored encrypted using your operating system's keychain.

We do not have a copy of these.

What we collect and keep

  • Account: your email address, and when the account was created.
  • Plan and billing status: which plan you are on, your billing period, whether extra usage is turned on and its limit, purchases of extra usage, and identifiers that link your account to our payment provider. We do not receive or store your card number.
  • Usage records: for each request to an AI model: the time, which provider and model, how many tokens were used and what it cost. For voice input: how many seconds of speech. These records do not contain your prompts, your code or the model's responses.
  • A device identifier: a random ID generated by the app on each computer, used to limit how many free accounts can be used on one machine. It is not derived from your hardware.
  • Support messages: anything you send to support@mirg.ai.

What passes through our servers but is not stored

When the agent works, the app sends your message, the conversation so far, the relevant parts of your project, and screenshots of your game to an AI model provider. These requests pass through our server so that we can check your plan and measure usage. We do not store their content, and we do not use it to train models.

Who receives your data

  • AI model providers: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (GPT and image generation) and Google (Gemini and image generation), depending on the model you choose. They receive the request content described above and handle it under their own terms and privacy policies for business API customers: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google.
  • ElevenLabs: when you use voice input, audio from your microphone is streamed from the app directly to ElevenLabs to be turned into text, only while the microphone button is active.
  • Stripe: processes payments. Stripe receives your payment details and email.
  • Supabase: hosts our account database and sign-in service.
  • Resend: delivers sign-in code emails.
  • GitHub: hosts app downloads and updates. The app checks GitHub for new versions, which reveals your IP address to GitHub.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Microphone: only if you use voice input.
  • Screen Recording and Accessibility (macOS): only if you ask the agent to look at or play a game that runs in its own window. Screenshots taken this way are of that game's window and are sent to the AI model provider as part of the request, like any other screenshot of your game.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics trackers and no crash reporting. This website does not use analytics or cookies.

How long we keep it

Account and billing records are kept while your account exists and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Usage records and support messages are currently kept for as long as your account exists, and are deleted when you ask us to delete your account, except where the law requires us to keep them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and associated data, by writing to support@mirg.ai. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights under local law, including the right to complain to a data protection authority. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Children

Mirg is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections are encrypted in transit. Our AI provider keys are held on our server and never sent to the app. Sign-in uses one-time codes sent by email rather than passwords. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee security.

Where data is processed

Our servers are in the United States, and the providers above may process data in other countries. If you use Mirg from outside the United States, your data will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
